I am Donald Stewart, the licensee of The Rowan public house at 8 High
Street, Carnaden. I have held the licence for eleven years.

I have been asked about the events of Friday evening, the 4th of
November 2022, in connection with the death of Ewan Drummond, whom
I knew as a fellow shopkeeper on the High Street though not as a
customer of the pub.

Robbie Thomson is a regular customer of The Rowan. He works at
Drummond Antiques next door but two along the High Street. He usually
comes in on a Friday and Saturday evening.

On the evening of Friday 4th November, Robbie came into the pub. I
would say it was around 7 o'clock when he arrived, but I cannot be
precise about the time. He came to the bar and I served him. He was
in his usual mood, nothing seemed unusual.

Robbie was in the pub for most of the evening as far as I can recall.
I was working behind the bar the whole night. It was a Friday and we
were rammed — I serve hundreds of pints on a Friday and I don't watch
individual customers.

I have been asked what time Robbie left the pub. I cannot say. I do
not remember him leaving. I do not recall him being there at closing
time, which was 11 o'clock that night. That is the honest position.
He may have left earlier and I did not notice.

I have no other information to add.

This statement is true to the best of my knowledge and belief.

Signed: D. Stewart
Date: 08/11/2022
