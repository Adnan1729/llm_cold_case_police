# The Carnaden Antiques Shop Killing

On the morning of Monday 7 November 2022, Ewan Drummond (63),
proprietor of Drummond Antiques, was found dead in the storeroom
of his shop on High Street, Carnaden, by his assistant Robbie
Thomson. Cause of death was blunt-force trauma to the back of the
head. Time of death was estimated between 21:00 on Friday 4
November and 03:00 on Saturday 5 November. Three items of stock
(a Victorian silver tea service, a walnut jewellery box, and a
silver-handled letter opener) were missing from the storeroom
inventory.

The initial investigation identified three persons of interest:
Calum Sinclair, a business associate in dispute with the deceased
over a property sale; Marion Drummond, the deceased's ex-wife,
with an outstanding financial claim; and Robbie Thomson, the
deceased's shop assistant. No arrest was made. The case was
closed as unsolved in mid-2023.
